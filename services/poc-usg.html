Real-time bedside imaging for faster decisions

We use an Esaote MyLab 40 HD sonography machine with a dedicated neonatal probe. All our neonatologists are trained in POC-USG, neurosonography and echocardiography, giving real-time information that is hard to assess clinically.

What's included

  • Esaote MyLab 40 HD with neonatal probe
  • Neurosonography
  • Functional echocardiography
  • Ultrasound-guided procedures
  • Earlier detection of complications
  • No need to move fragile babies

Safer procedures

Real-time sonography helps us perform invasive procedures safely and prevent complications.
